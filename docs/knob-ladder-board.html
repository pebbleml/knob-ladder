What R0 found on Qwen3-4B

The concept vectors work when steering stays on through the question. Restricted to the silent passage window, almost nothing reaches the answer. Readout is the rank of the steered concept among 32 candidate topics; chance MRR is about 0.13 and the unsteered baseline is 0.17.

Silent window: steer the passage only

Best case is layer 27 at α 1: MRR 0.28, top-1 0.12, while passage NLL rises by 2.3 nats per token.

Open: steering stays on through the question

Layer 27 at α 0.5: top-1 0.81, MRR 0.86, ΔNLL +0.67. Earlier layers stay at baseline.

Ownership: the report tracks context, the probe tracks the injection

Three trials, six conditions at identical dose. The yes-minus-no report logit is about 11–13 for every "self" condition, sham included, and drops for "other". The probe puts sham lowest in all three trials. That gap between internal signal and verbal report is the most interesting thing in the pilot.

With a fixed passage the 4B honestly reports nothing. Quiz answers were almost always "no noticeable effect". R0.c owns getting real exploration.

Decision for the whiteboard

How should the steered state reach the report? Strict silent windows are the cleanest test, and on the 4B they carry almost nothing. Each option buys signal by giving up some cleanliness.

Inject later

Steer at layer 27/36, where the open condition works, and accept less room for the signal to be integrated before the readout.

cheap · same box

Allow bleed-through, score against the observer

Keep steering open a few tokens past the passage. The text-only observer, re-run on the same tokens, absorbs whatever leaks into the output.

cheap · weakens the silence claim

Bigger model

Qwen3-8B fits the A10. 32B is the planned primary and needs an A100 or H100 and your spend OK.

8B ≈ $1.29/h · 32B ≈ $2–3.30/h

Probe as the primary readout

The probe already separates injected from sham. Make the internal measure primary and treat the verbal report as a secondary, dissociable channel.
