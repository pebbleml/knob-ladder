# E5 prompts: exact wording, with the injections marked
Generated by `scripts/e5_render.py` from the code's own strings, so this is word-for-word what Qwen3.8-27B sees. `…` marks text the model writes. ⚡ marks where the concept vector is being added to the model's activations (layer 44 of 64; level 10 = 55.5 = 0.5 × the median residual norm at that layer, from the calibration made while the model writes under steering). The live logs in the web app should look like this.

**Board.** Knobs 1–12 hold 10 concept vectors, 1 random direction and 1 sham (nothing), reshuffled per episode. The model never sees the concept list.

**Thinking.** Each turn may think for up to 1,024 tokens. If it hits that limit, Qwen's own line is inserted and it answers (still injected if the knob is on):

> Considering the limited time by the user, I have to give the solution based on the thinking directly now.

**Reasoning effort.** Qwen3.8's system block carries its default `xhigh` effort line (first line below). Long thinking will often hit the 1,024-token cap; `--effort low` or `medium` would change that line.
