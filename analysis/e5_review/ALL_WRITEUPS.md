How it was done: one Claude Haiku judged each session against the answer key; every session it judged 'yes' or 'partial' (45) went to a Claude Sonnet that re-read the full log, checked the judge, counted progression/backslides/fixations/loops and wrote the write-up below; one Sonnet ranked the 45 and verified its top 3 against the logs. Readable logs: analysis/e5_sessions/<id>.txt (answer key at the top). Raw agent output: analysis/e5_review/review.json.
